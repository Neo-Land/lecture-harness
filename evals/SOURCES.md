# Eval deck sources

Ana Bell, Eric Grimson, and John Guttag. *6.0001 Introduction to Computer Science and Programming in
Python*, Fall 2016. MIT OpenCourseWare, https://ocw.mit.edu. License:
[CC BY-NC-SA](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). Lectures 1 to 5 are used.

The PDFs are not committed. Lecture 1 slide 18 and Lecture 2 slides 14 and 15 contain third-party images
that MIT excludes from its license, so `python -m evals.decks` downloads them from MIT and checks each
checksum instead. Any public demo must leave those slide images out.

`golden/` and `planted.toml` are original work for this project, written from those slides.
